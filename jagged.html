Jagged

19 April 2026 · 1 min read

Everyone talks about AI like it’s a rising tide, lifting all boats equally. It isn’t.

Last year, researchers from Harvard and BCG ran an experiment. They gave consultants real tasks and split them into two groups, one with AI, one without. The results were strange. On certain tasks, AI made even average consultants perform like experts. But push slightly outside that zone, and the same consultants started confidently producing wrong answers. The performance cliff was sharp, sudden, invisible.

They called it the jagged frontier.

I’m seventeen. I’m figuring out which skills are actually worth building. And this study unsettled me more than any career advice ever has, because the frontier isn’t labelled. Nobody tells you which side of the line you’re standing on.

The consultants who got worse with AI weren’t careless. They just couldn’t see the edge.

That’s the real question my generation has to answer. Not “will AI take my job” but “am I building the judgment to know when not to trust it?”

The tide isn’t rising equally. It’s jagged.
