Bootstrap

8 February 2026 · 1 min read

I am not sure when exactly I became the person my dad calls when something at the factory or office needs fixing. It just happened.

He runs a paint and door manufacturing business, and he is also into real estate. Good products, loyal clients, years of experience across these connected businesses. But the back end was a mess in every one of them. Nobody knew what they were doing each day. Leads were scattered across WhatsApp chats. Marketing was word of mouth and nothing else. No social media presence, no functioning website, and plenty more missing.

I started fixing it with AI. Organised the leads, built a system so everyone knew their tasks, designed them a website, and used AI to build their social media presence from scratch. Nothing fancy. Just things that should have existed already.

But while doing that, I kept thinking about how many businesses like his are out there. Founders who know their craft but have no roadmap. No timeline, no marketing plan, no one telling them what to do on day one versus day thirty. And a lot of them are not even looking for growth because nobody ever showed them what growth could look like.

That’s why I built BuildSmart. A tool that sits next to a first-time founder and actually guides them. Day by day. Marketing, planning, finances, government schemes, all of it.

Not because it’s a good startup idea. Because I watched my dad not have it for years.
